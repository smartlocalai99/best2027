In pregnancy, go to hospital straight away if you have

  • bleeding from the vagina
  • a fit (convulsion)
  • a severe headache with blurred vision
  • a fever and feel too weak to get out of bed
  • severe pain in the belly
  • fast or difficult breathing
  • noticed your baby moving less than usual

Based on the World Health Organization’s danger signs in pregnancy and NHS advice on baby movements. Emergency care at Dhruva Hospitals is open 24 hours. Bleeding in early pregnancy and high BP in pregnancy explain more.

Questions families ask

Who is the best gynecologist in Kadapa?

Look for a gynaecologist with a postgraduate qualification in obstetrics and gynaecology who works in a hospital with an operation theatre and a NICU. At Dhruva Hospitals that is Dr. S Mounika Reddy (MBBS, MS OBG), with further fellowships in reproductive medicine, minimal access surgery and fetal scans.

Which is the best maternity hospital in Kadapa?

For delivery, the hospital should have a gynaecologist, an operation theatre for caesarean section and a NICU in the same building. Dhruva Hospitals has all three, with painless labour options for normal delivery.

Do you offer painless normal delivery?

Yes, painless labour options are available for normal delivery. Your gynaecologist will discuss whether they suit you during your antenatal visits. Read about painless delivery.

Is there a fertility centre in Kadapa?

Dhruva Hospitals offers fertility tests and treatment, including semen analysis, ovulation study, IUI, IVF and ICSI, led by Dr. S Mounika Reddy. Fertility treatment in Kadapa.

Do you treat PCOD and irregular periods?

Yes. PCOD / PCOS, heavy, painful, irregular or missed periods, fibroids and ovarian cysts are all treated in the gynaecology department.
