Is there a 24-hour hospital near me in Kadapa?

Dhruva Hospitals, beside Raithu Bazar near Sandhya Circle, has emergency care open 24 hours, every day, for adults, children and newborns.

Which hospital is near Sandhya Circle, Kadapa?

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any earlier prescriptions and test reports, a list of the medicines you take and an ID such as Aadhaar. For pregnancy, bring your antenatal card and scan reports; for a child, bring the vaccination card.
