How to choose the best hospital in Kadapa

The best hospital is the one that fits what your family needs. These are the questions worth asking any hospital before you need it, and how Dhruva Hospitals answers them.

  • Is emergency care open at night?Yes. Emergency care at Dhruva Hospitals is open 24 hours, every day, including for children and newborns.
  • If my newborn needs special care, will we have to move hospitals?The NICU is in the same building as the delivery rooms, with CPAP, HFNC and ventilator support, so a newborn who needs help can be cared for on site.
  • Who are the specialists, and what are their qualifications?Each senior doctor’s qualifications are listed above and on the doctors page, including DNB Paediatrics, MS (OBG) and MD General Medicine from JIPMER.
  • Is the hospital registered with the state?Yes. Registered under the A.P. Allopathic Private Medical Care Establishments Act, 2002, file no. 01-325, valid until 16 June 2031.
  • Are tests, scans and medicines available inside?Yes. There is an in-house lab, digital X-ray, ultrasound and Doppler, ECG and a pharmacy.
  • Can I book without travelling first?Yes. Call +91 99599 59694 or WhatsApp the same number.

Questions families ask

Which is the best hospital in Kadapa for delivery?

Look for a hospital where an obstetrician, an operation theatre for caesarean section and a NICU are all in the same building, so neither mother nor baby has to be moved in an emergency. Dhruva Hospitals has all three. Dr. S Mounika Reddy leads pregnancy care and Dr. Sudarshan Reddy K leads newborn care.

Is there a children’s hospital near me in Kadapa?

Dhruva Hospitals, beside Raithu Bazar near Sandhya Circle, has a paediatrician-led children’s department, a children’s ward and a NICU. Paediatric emergency care is open 24 hours. See children’s care at Dhruva Hospitals.

What are the OPD timings at Dhruva Hospitals?

Outpatient consultations run 9 AM – 8 PM. Emergency care is open 24 hours, every day. Call +91 99599 59694 to check when a particular doctor is available.

How do I book an appointment?

Call +91 99599 59694 or +91 99599 59693, or send a WhatsApp message. There is no online form; the front desk books you in over the phone.

Where exactly is Dhruva Hospitals in Kadapa?

Venu Gopal House, S Reddy Hospital, 1/705-1, Beside Raithu Bazar, Dwaraka Nagar, Old, Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh 516001. It is the corner building beside Raithu Bazar, near Sandhya Circle (shown as Pylon Circle on Google Maps). Directions and landmarks.

Is Dhruva Hospitals a registered hospital?

Yes. It is registered under the A.P. Allopathic Private Medical Care Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2002, file no. 01-325, valid until 16 June 2031, for 20 beds.
