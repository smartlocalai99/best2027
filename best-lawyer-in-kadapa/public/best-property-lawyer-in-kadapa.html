Documents to gather for a land or property case

Bring whatever you have to the first meeting. Copies are fine to start with.

  • Sale deed, gift deed or partition deed for the property
  • Earlier (link) documents showing how ownership passed
  • Encumbrance certificate (EC)
  • Pattadar passbook and title deed
  • Adangal and 1-B (ROR) extracts
  • Survey number details and FMB sketch
  • Property or house tax receipts
  • Any legal notice, police complaint or court papers

Who is the best property lawyer in Kadapa?

For a land or property dispute, look for an advocate who knows both the civil courts and the revenue side, because many Kadapa disputes involve records as well as title. Rajesh Kumar Reddy has handled civil, property and revenue matters in Kadapa since 1990.

What is an injunction?

An injunction is a court order stopping someone from doing something, such as interfering with your possession of land. A temporary injunction can be granted while the suit is pending; a permanent injunction is granted in the final decree. Injunction suits in Kadapa.

Can a mistake in the land records (Adangal / 1-B) be corrected?

Yes. Errors in revenue records can be corrected by applying to the revenue authorities, with appeals available if the application is refused. Where the dispute is about who owns the land, a civil suit may also be needed. Revenue matters in Kadapa.

Should I get the title checked before buying land?

Yes. A title check traces ownership through earlier deeds and the encumbrance certificate, and confirms the seller has the right to sell before you pay. Title verification in Kadapa.

How is family property divided?

Co-owners can divide property by agreement, through a registered partition deed. If they cannot agree, any co-owner can file a partition suit in the civil court. Partition suits in Kadapa.
