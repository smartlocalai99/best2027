Bring your child to hospital straight away if they

  • cannot drink or breastfeed
  • vomit everything they take
  • have had a fit (convulsion)
  • are unusually sleepy or hard to wake
  • are breathing fast or with difficulty, or the chest pulls in with each breath
  • are younger than 2 months and have a fever, feel cold, or are not feeding well

Questions families ask

Who is the best pediatrician in Kadapa?

The right paediatrician for your family is qualified in paediatrics, reachable when your child is unwell, and backed by a hospital that can admit children and newborns. At Dhruva Hospitals that is Dr. Sudarshan Reddy K (MBBS, DNB Paediatrics, IAP Fellowship in Neonatology), formerly a consultant at RIMS Kadapa.

Is there a NICU in Kadapa for premature babies?

Yes. Dhruva Hospitals has a NICU with CPAP, HFNC and ventilator support, phototherapy for jaundice and care for babies born before 37 weeks. It is in the same building as the delivery rooms.

Is a children’s doctor available at night?

The hospital’s neonatal and paediatric emergency team is on duty day and night. If your child is seriously unwell, come straight in.

Do you give baby vaccinations?

Yes. Vaccinations follow the Indian Academy of Paediatrics (IAP) schedule. Bring your child’s vaccination card to each visit. See the vaccination schedule.

Can my child be admitted at Dhruva Hospitals?

Yes. There is a children’s ward with round-the-clock nursing, and intensive care with ventilator support for children who are critically ill.
