Mutual consent divorce: how it works

Under Section 13B of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955. Marriages under the Special Marriage Act follow a similar process under Section 28.

  1. Live separately for at least a year and agree to divorce, including on maintenance, custody and property.
  2. File a joint petition in the Family Court (the first motion).
  3. Wait six months. The court can waive this waiting period in suitable cases.
  4. Confirm at the second motion, and the court grants the divorce decree.

How long does a mutual consent divorce take?

After the joint petition is filed, the law sets a six-month waiting period before the second motion. The court can waive this in suitable cases, and the Supreme Court has held that the waiting period is not mandatory.

Can I get maintenance while the case is going on?

Yes. A spouse without enough income can ask for interim maintenance and litigation expenses while the case is pending, for example under Section 24 of the Hindu Marriage Act. Separate maintenance claims can be made under Section 144 of the BNSS. Maintenance cases in Kadapa.

Who gets custody of the children?

The court decides custody on the welfare of the child, considering age, schooling, care and stability. The other parent is usually given visitation. Child custody in Kadapa.

Are family court cases kept private?

Under the Family Courts Act, 1984, the court can hold proceedings in private (in camera) if it wishes or if either party asks. Your consultation with the advocate is confidential.
